## Week 11 topics

1. Meaning of design refinement; analysis model → design model
2. Analysis class vs design class; entity, boundary and control classes
3. Refining class attributes and operations (signatures)
4. Responsibility-driven design (assignment of responsibilities)
5. Cohesion and coupling (high cohesion, low coupling via interfaces)
6. Interfaces (contract, wall-socket analogy) and abstract classes (comparison)
7. Association vs inheritance (HAS / IS-A test)
8. Encapsulation and information hiding
9. Dependencies and dependency injection
10. Separation of concerns and layered design
11. Design for reuse, design for change, and design quality
12. Design traceability (R01 example)
13. Integrated case study (seven steps); common design mistakes
14. Practical class, tutorial, examination questions and references
