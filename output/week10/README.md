## Week 10 topics

1. Overview of design — what software design and OO design mean
2. Analysis vs design (WHAT vs HOW); analysis model vs design model
3. Why design is necessary; the eight design goals
4. Responsibility (knowing vs doing) and responsibility assignment
5. Cohesion and coupling — high cohesion + low coupling
6. Abstraction, encapsulation, separation of concerns
7. Design architecture (three layers) and refinement
8. Combining the Object, Dynamic and Behavioural models (case study, 7 steps)
9. Model consistency and traceability
10. Sequence diagrams, design classes and interfaces in design
11. Design patterns and SOLID (outline)
12. The complete OO design process; practical class; tutorial and exam questions; references
