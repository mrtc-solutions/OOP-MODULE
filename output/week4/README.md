## Week 4 topics

1. Week 4 introduction and learning outcomes
2. The five questions UML helps you answer
3. UML building blocks — things, relationships, diagrams
4. Structural things (class, interface, object, component, node)
5. Behavioural, grouping and annotational things
6. The four relationships — association, dependency, generalization, realization
7. When a relationship needs its own class (association class)
8. UML diagrams and the model-vs-diagram distinction
9. Rules of UML — syntax, semantics, well-formedness
10. The seven-question quality checklist
11. Common mechanisms — specifications, adornments, divisions, extensibility
12. Adornments: visibility symbols
13. Extensibility — stereotypes, tagged values, constraints, profiles
14. UML architecture — the M0–M3 meta-modelling hierarchy
15. Model vs diagram, multiple views, abstraction and packages
16. Common student mistakes (generalization direction, symbol misuse, over-classification)
17. Case study — university e-learning system
18. Practical / tutorial activities and examination-style questions
19. References (OMG UML 2.5.1 as primary source)
