## Week 12 topics

1. Meaning of integrated modelling; the house analogy
2. Why models must be consistent; different UML models, different views
3. The big picture: requirements → models → integrated design → implementation
4. Case study — university course registration (actors, use cases, classes)
5. Traceability; sequence diagrams and consistency checking
6. State modelling; activity diagrams; comparing the behavioural views
7. Integrating the four models; the consistency checklist
8. Multiplicity; packages for organising large models
9. Architectural integration (layers) and component thinking
10. Design validation; the design-review questions
11. Model vs diagram (Google Maps analogy)
12. The complete integrated example (R01); tutorial, practical and exam questions; references
