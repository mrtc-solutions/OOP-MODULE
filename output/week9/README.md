## Week 9 topics

1. Software development process — why it is needed; UP vs RUP
2. Iterative vs incremental development; iterations and increments
3. The four characteristics — use-case driven, architecture-centred, iterative & incremental, risk-driven
4. The four phases — Inception, Elaboration, Construction, Transition (and their milestones)
5. Phases contain iterations (UP is not waterfall)
6. Process workflows/disciplines and their intensity across phases
7. Artefacts across the phases
8. Reuse in OO development — assets and evaluation guidelines
9. Generalisation vs inheritance; concurrency
10. Connecting UP to the UML models; the Mzuni case study
11. Practical lab, tutorial, common misunderstandings, exam questions, references
