AIM OF COURSE 	:
The aim of this module is to introduce students to the Object-Oriented model as an aid to understanding a system under study and representing the system’s requirements. 

LEARNING OUTCOMES
Upon completion of the course, the learner shall be able to:
Discuss the strengths and limitations of the object-oriented approach to systems development.
Design system models using UML.
Build an object-oriented model of a given system
Express the value of designing a multilayer system built of reusable and adaptable components.
